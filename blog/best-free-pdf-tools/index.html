The 7 Best Free PDF Tools in 2026 (Online & Offline)

Published August 30, 2026 · Updated September 13, 2026 · 8 min read

Nobody should pay for Adobe Acrobat to merge two PDFs. The free PDF ecosystem in 2026 is excellent — the trick is knowing which tool has no watermark, no page limit and no surprise “upgrade to continue”. We merged, split, compressed and converted our way through all of them. These seven survived.

1. iLovePDF — the best all-rounder

Merge, split, compress, Office⇄PDF conversion, watermark, rotate, unlock and e-sign in one tidy interface. The free web tier handles the overwhelming majority of everyday tasks without a watermark, and there are desktop and mobile apps too.

2. PDF24 — no limits, really

Over a hundred tools in the browser with no meaningful daily caps, plus a free Windows app for offline and privacy-sensitive work. The interface looks a decade older than iLovePDF’s — the absence of limits more than compensates.

3. Stirling PDF — the privacy-first power tool

An open-source, self-hosted toolbox with 50+ operations that never leaves your machine. If you handle contracts, invoices or anything sensitive — and can run one Docker container — this is the most professional free option that exists.

4. Sejda — the one that edits text properly

Most “PDF editors” just slap annotations on top. Sejda edits existing paragraph text in place, which still feels like magic. Free quota is 3 tasks per hour — enough for the occasional deep edit.

5. Smallpdf — the prettiest quick fixes

Fast, clean and friendly with a rotating set of free daily tasks. For a one-off compress or convert on someone else’s computer, it’s the tool we open first.

6. PDFescape — form rescue

Fills and annotates PDF forms in the browser — the instant fix when someone sends a “fillable” form that isn’t.

7. Adobe Acrobat Online — the fidelity guarantee

Adobe’s free online converters, compressor and e-sign tools follow the PDF spec perfectly. When a converted document must look exactly right — government forms, legal filings — go straight to the source.

Which free PDF tool should you pick?

  • Everyday tasks: iLovePDF, with PDF24 when you hit limits.
  • Sensitive documents: Stirling PDF self-hosted, or PDF24’s desktop app.
  • Real text editing: Sejda.
  • Maximum fidelity: Adobe’s own online tools.

Frequently asked questions

Is iLovePDF actually free, or will I hit paywalls?

Core tasks — merge, split, compress and convert — are free without watermarks, with daily limits on some operations. Subscriptions mainly unlock batch processing and automation, not the basics.

Which free PDF tool is safest for confidential documents?

Self-hosted Stirling PDF or PDF24’s offline Windows app — files never leave your machine. Browser-based tools upload documents to their servers, however briefly, so treat them as off-limits for sensitive contracts.

Can a free tool really edit the text inside a PDF?

Yes — Sejda edits existing paragraph text in place, not just annotations. Most other free tools only overlay comments or form fields.

Do I still need to pay for Adobe Acrobat?

Only for heavy professional workflows like advanced form design or redaction. For conversions, compression and e-signatures, Adobe’s own free online tools already cover the fidelity-critical cases.
