12 Best Free SEO Tools (Ranked for 2026)

Published September 6, 2026 · 11 min read

Enterprise SEO suites cost more per month than most domains earn per year. The secret nobody advertises: for a brand-new or small site, the free tier of everything — crawl, keywords, backlinks, performance — is enough to reach your first meaningful traffic. Here’s the stack we’d assemble in 2026, in the order you’ll need it.

1. Google Search Console — the foundation

Indexing status, the actual queries that bring impressions, click-through rates and Core Web Vitals, straight from the source. Install it before you do anything else, and submit your sitemap on day one.

2. Backlink Submit — free backlinks, the honest way

Backlinks remain the hardest part of early SEO. Backlink Submit attacks the boring part: a curated, verified database of 3,840+ free backlink sites — directories, startup launchpads, profile and bookmarking sources — plus a browser extension that autofills every submission form. What used to be a week of copy-paste becomes an afternoon of clicking “submit”. For a new site looking for its first fifty links, this is the single highest-leverage free tool on this list.

3. Ahrefs Webmaster Tools — free audit of your own site

The famous crawler, pointed at your own property for free: backlink profile, organic keywords and an SEO health report that prioritises fixes. Combine it with #1 and you have most of a paid suite.

4. Bing Webmaster Tools — the free extras

Bing’s console quietly includes keyword research, a site scan and index control — and its index powers Yahoo and DuckDuckGo too. Most competitors ignore it; that’s exactly why you shouldn’t.

5. Google Keyword Planner — volumes from the source

Free with any Google Ads account (no spend required). Ranges are coarse, but the data comes from the same auction that powers ads — and nothing beats it for validating demand before you write.

6. Screaming Frog — the classic crawler

The free licence crawls up to 500 URLs — enough for most small sites — and surfaces broken links, redirect chains, thin metas and duplicates in minutes.

7. PageSpeed Insights — speed is table stakes

Lab data plus real-user Core Web Vitals with prioritised fixes. When a page loads slowly, this tells you why in plain language.

8. Rich Results Test — win the fancy SERP features

Validate your structured data and preview which rich results you’re eligible for — FAQ dropdowns, stars, breadcrumbs. Free visibility is sitting on the table.

9. Ubersuggest — keywords with a daily free quota

Keyword volumes, difficulty and content ideas with enough free daily uses to run a small site’s entire planning workflow.

10. Google Trends — timing is ranking

Compare interest across terms and regions before committing a month of writing to a topic. Seasonality hides in plain sight here.

11. AnswerThePublic — the question mine

Free daily searches turn any seed keyword into the actual questions people ask — the raw material for FAQ sections and long-tail posts.

12. Keyword Surfer — volumes in your search bar

A free Chrome extension that shows search volumes right in the Google results page. Zero workflow change, permanent utility.

The free stack, assembled

Wire it together: plan with Keyword Planner and Trends, publish, audit with Ahrefs WT and Screaming Frog, build your first links with Backlink Submit, then watch Search Console tell you what to double down on. Total cost: $0.
