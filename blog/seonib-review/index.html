What is SEONIB?

SEONIB is an AI blog-automation pipeline aimed squarely at e-commerce and content sites. Its pitch: “Import product links, automatically generate articles and publish them to continuously gain organic traffic.” The important word is pipeline — this isn’t another AI writing box that hands you text and wishes you luck. It covers topic discovery, generation, scheduling and distribution as one connected flow, with an explicit eye on AEO (showing up in AI search results) as well as classic SEO.

The pipeline: four modules

1. Trend discovery

The system monitors industry trends in real time, looks for competitor content gaps, weighs keyword search volume and pushes topic suggestions into a queue. In other words, the “what should we write about?” meeting is automated.

2. Content generation

Multiple input-to-article modes (more below) across 40+ languages — notable for sellers outside the English-first markets most AI writers assume.

3. Scheduled publishing

Daily, weekly or custom cadence: the calendar fills itself and posts go out without anyone clicking publish, with a content-calendar view for previewing and adjusting what’s coming.

4. Multi-platform sync

One publish fans out to every connected platform, with webhooks for custom setups.

Five ways to turn input into articles

The generation modes map to how e-commerce teams actually work:

  • Product to Blog — paste a product URL, get a buyer’s guide, tutorial or review. The flagship mode for stores.
  • Keyword to Blog — classic keyword-targeted articles for search demand you already know.
  • Trend to Blog — ride what the trend module just spotted.
  • Social to Blog — turn content from Twitter/X, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn into articles.
  • Link to Blog — feed it any link as raw material.

There’s also a bold on-ramp for people without a site at all: enter a domain and SEONIB can stand up a content site in about ten minutes.

Where it publishes

Destinations cover the usual suspects — WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Shopline, Wix, Ghost, Medium and Contentful — plus webhooks for anything custom. Interestingly, it also publishes to AI app-builder platforms (Bolt.new, Lovable, Replit, Base44, v0, Framer), which makes sense in 2026: plenty of new stores now live on sites vibe-built in an afternoon.

The honest take

We like: the full-pipeline design (generation without scheduling is half a product), the genuinely global language support, publishing targets that match where e-commerce actually lives, and the AEO framing — AI search results are becoming a real traffic source.

Watch-outs: it’s a young product with a free trial and no public plan pricing on the homepage, so check current terms before committing. And automation without oversight is how stores end up with twenty interchangeable posts — keep a human review step in the calendar, inject real product knowledge the AI can’t know, and remember that content alone doesn’t rank. Verdict

4 out of 5. SEONIB is the most complete “content engine in a box” we’ve seen aimed at e-commerce — discovery to distribution in one loop, in 40+ languages. Frequently asked questions

Is SEONIB free to use?

There is a free trial you can start without a card. Full pricing isn’t published on the homepage, so check the site’s pricing page for current plan details before committing.

Will Google penalise AI-generated blog content?

Google judges content by quality and helpfulness, not by how it was produced. The practical risk is low-effort bulk publishing — so treat SEONIB’s drafts as first passes, keep a human review step, and add your own product knowledge before publishing.

Which platforms can SEONIB publish to?

WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Shopline, Wix, Ghost, Medium and Contentful, plus webhooks for custom systems. It can also push to sites built with AI app builders like Bolt.new, Lovable, Replit, Base44, v0 and Framer.

Do I need SEO experience to use SEONIB?

No — that’s the point of the pipeline. Trend discovery suggests the topics, generation modes handle the writing, and scheduled publishing keeps the calendar full. You review and approve rather than research and draft.
