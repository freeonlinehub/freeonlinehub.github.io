“Free online video editor” usually comes with an asterisk the size of the paywall: upload your footage to someone’s server, wait in a render queue, and find your watermark at export. What is VideoEditorOnline?

VideoEditorOnline is a free video editor that runs entirely in a web browser — no install, no account for the core workflow, and no upload step. Under the hood it processes your footage locally with hardware acceleration, which is why a browser editor can feel snappier than some desktop apps on a decent machine. It covers the everyday editing jobs: trimming, cutting and merging clips, adding text and music, filters and transitions, subtitles, resizing and GIF conversion.

What you can do in it

  • The essentials, done properly: trim, cut and merge clips on a timeline, with music tracks and filters layered on top.
  • Real subtitle support: add text manually or import existing SRT/VTT files — genuinely useful if you caption separately and just need them burned in.
  • Social-ready resizing: one-click aspect presets for TikTok and Reels (9:16), YouTube (16:9) and square Facebook posts (1:1), so one edit feeds every feed.
  • Exports without games: MP4 or WebM at 1080p, 720p or 480p, plus GIF conversion for the places video autoplay can’t.

The client-side difference

Here’s the part we care about most, and why this tool earned its Editor’s Pick: the site states plainly that your videos stay on your device and are never uploaded to a server. For client work under NDA, internal training footage, or simply your family clips, that changes the calculus completely — the convenience of a browser tool without handing raw footage to a third party. It also means no upload waits and no render queues; export speed is your machine’s, not a shared server’s.

The Pro tier, honestly

The free editor is not a cripple-ware demo — everything listed above works without paying. There is one paid extra: AI HD Export, which upscales and enhances output for sharper, higher-resolution results. That’s a fair line to draw: the editing is free, the AI-quality boost is the premium. If your footage is already crisp, you may never need it.

The same ecosystem also offers 26+ companion AI tools — watermark removal, translation, upscaling, avatar and voiceover generation, background replacement — if you want to extend the pipeline later.

Frequently asked questions

Is VideoEditorOnline really free?

Yes — the core editor (trimming, cutting, merging, subtitles, music, filters, aspect-ratio resizing and exports up to 1080p) is free with no subscription. A Pro tier exists for one specific extra: AI HD Export, which upscales and enhances output quality.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Editing and export happen locally in your browser with hardware acceleration — the site states plainly that your footage stays on your device and is never uploaded. That also means no waiting in an upload or render queue.

Can I add subtitles, and does it support SRT files?

Yes. You can add text and subtitles manually, or import existing SRT or VTT subtitle files directly — handy if you caption your videos separately and just need them burned in.

What formats and resolutions can I export?

Exports come out as MP4 or WebM at 1080p, 720p or 480p, with one-click aspect-ratio presets for TikTok and Reels (9:16), YouTube (16:9) and square Facebook posts (1:1). GIF conversion is supported too.
