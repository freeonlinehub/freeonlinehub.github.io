VideosPrompt Review: Find Proven AI Video Prompts

Published September 13, 2026 · 8 min read

Every AI video generator charges for attempts, which turns prompt writing into an expensive guessing game. VideosPrompt attacks that exact pain point: a community library where every prompt arrives attached to the video it actually produced. We spent time inside the gallery this week. Here’s how it works and who it’s for.

What is VideosPrompt?

VideosPrompt calls itself an AI video prompt community, and its tagline says everything about the philosophy: “Discover. Copy. Remix. Create.” — or as the site puts it more bluntly, watch a video and steal the prompt to make it your own. Instead of another text-to-video generator, it’s the layer underneath: a browsable, searchable feed of prompts that have already produced good video, with new additions daily.

Each prompt card shows what you actually want to know before spending credits: a star rating, a view count and a copy count. Popularity is a quality signal — a prompt copied hundreds of times is a prompt that works.

How it works: discover, copy, remix

The flow is deliberately friction-free:

  1. Filter by model. Pick the generator you’re using — prompts are tuned per model, and a Kling prompt structure is not a Veo prompt structure.
  2. Watch the proof. Open a detail page and see the video the prompt produced before you commit.
  3. Copy or remix. Take the prompt as-is, or tweak the subject, style and camera moves for your own scene.
  4. Give back. Submit your own winners to the community (submission needs a sign-in; browsing doesn’t).

Model coverage

The filter list reads like a 2026 AI-video shopping aisle: Veo, Kling, Sora, Runway, Hailuo, Wan, Seedance, Veonib, Gemini Omni and MiniMax H3. That’s the practical value of a community library — each model family has its own prompt grammar, and the gallery lets you learn the grammar of whichever model you’re paying for, from people already fluent in it.

Where VideosPrompt gets powerful is as part of a pipeline rather than alone:

  • Selling products? Skip prompt-writing entirely for e-commerce: VEONIB turns a product URL into a finished marketing video in about a minute. VideosPrompt is what you reach for when you want full creative control instead.
  • Editing and publishing. Whatever you generate drops straight into the free editors from our video tools category.

What we like, what could improve

We like: the proof-before-you-pay model (video attached to every prompt), the per-model filtering, popularity signals that make good prompts findable, and a genuinely free browsing experience — no account needed to consume the library.

Could improve: submitting requires a sign-in, which will throttle some community growth; and as the library grows, tag-based search beyond model filters will matter. Neither is unusual for a young community product — and daily additions suggest the flywheel is already turning.

Verdict: 4.5 out of 5. If you generate AI video regularly, an hour inside VideosPrompt will save you more credits than any tutorial. Start with the Hot feed for your model, remix three prompts, and you’ll already have a personal style bank. Frequently asked questions

Is VideosPrompt free to use?

Yes — browsing, filtering, watching the sample videos and copying prompts are all free. Submitting your own prompts requires a sign-in, but consuming the library does not.

Which AI video models does VideosPrompt support?

The gallery can be filtered by model, including Veo, Kling, Sora, Runway, Hailuo, Wan, Seedance, Veonib, Gemini Omni and MiniMax H3 — the models most creators actually generate with in 2026.

How is VideosPrompt different from just writing my own prompts?

Every entry shows the actual video the prompt produced, along with community ratings and copy counts. You start from prompts that are proven to work instead of paying credits to learn what doesn’t.

Can I turn a video I found on social media into a prompt?

Yes — pair VideosPrompt with SocialToPrompt. SocialToPrompt reverse-engineers any TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook or X video into a detailed AI video prompt you can save, remix and share back to the community.
